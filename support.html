Good to know

How do I play?

Look at how the pieces connect. Drag to rotate open rings, or slide movable shapes, then free the pieces in the right order. Follow the first-level gesture guide to get started.

What can I do when I’m stuck?

Try another angle or restart the level. A hint can show a useful move; the magic wand lets you choose a piece to remove. Extra help may be available through an optional rewarded image ad.

How do the 10-second ads work?

The image must stay open for 10 seconds of foreground time to earn the requested reward. You can close early, but you will not receive the reward. Background time does not count. You never need to click the ad or install another app.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The 60 levels and ad images are bundled with the game. Opening external support pages or an advertised App Store page requires an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is no game account or cloud save. Deleting the app or changing devices may remove your progress and unlocked cosmetics. Contact us before reinstalling if you are trying to troubleshoot an issue.

Can I turn analytics off?

Yes. Analytics is off until you choose Allow Analytics. At any time, open Home → Settings → Privacy & Support to change your choice. Gameplay and rewards are the same either way.

How do I request access to or deletion of my data?

Use Contact Support / Data Request in the game’s Privacy & Support panel, or email protect100for+huan@gmail.com. The in-game email draft can include the analytics reference needed to find records. Turning analytics off or deleting the app does not automatically delete previously uploaded data. See our Privacy Policy.

Does the game have purchases or subscriptions?

No. The first release has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It includes optional rewarded image ads.
