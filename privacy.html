Privacy Policy

Your game. Your choice about analytics.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Analytics starts off. Choose “Not Now” to play without optional gameplay analytics. You can change this at Home → Settings → Privacy & Support.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Clay Unloop development team (“we,” “us”) handles information in Clay Unloop: Ring Puzzle and on this website. Contact us at protect100for+huan@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Clay Unloop is a single-player puzzle game. No account is required. The app does not ask for your name, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone access, or precise location.

2. Information on your device

Game progress, cosmetic unlocks, sound settings, and your analytics choice are saved locally. If you allow analytics, an app-specific random installation identifier and a bounded event queue are also stored locally. There is no in-game account or cloud-save service.

Deleting the app may remove local progress and settings, subject to your device’s backup and restore behavior. It does not automatically delete analytics records already uploaded.

3. Optional gameplay analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase is disabled until you choose Allow Analytics. If you opt in, we use it to understand active users, return visits, play time, level attempts and completion, use of help, and interaction with optional image ads. This helps us improve the game.

Events can include an app-specific installation ID, Firebase app-instance ID, session and event IDs, app version, device and operating-system details, level numbers, move counts, and durations. Firebase may derive approximate region from your IP address. We do not request the device’s location permission.

These identifiers can associate events from the same installation; they are not a named user account. We do not request IDFA or use IDFV for analytics. The app denies advertising-storage, ad-user-data, and ad-personalization consent. We do not use this gameplay analytics for personalized ads or sell it.

Google processes analytics under its applicable service terms. Read Firebase’s privacy and security information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Optional ads and external links

Rewarded image ads are selected from images bundled with the game. Selection does not use a personal advertising profile, and these placements do not use an advertising-network SDK. If analytics is enabled, events record whether a placement started, completed, was closed, or was clicked.

Tapping an ad or its Learn More button opens the advertised app’s App Store page. The store and the advertised app’s operator have their own terms and privacy practices. Visiting the store or installing another app is never necessary to earn a reward.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer you, investigate the issue, or handle your data request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unrelated sensitive information.

The in-game Contact Support / Data Request action prepares an editable email containing the app version and your analytics reference, if one exists. Nothing is sent until you send that email.

6. Your choices and rights

You can play with analytics off, with the same gameplay and rewards. Open Home → Settings → Privacy & Support to change your choice. Turning analytics off stops further gameplay-event collection, disables Firebase Analytics, resets its local analytics state, and clears our local event queue. Previously uploaded records are not deleted automatically.

To ask about, access, correct, or delete information associated with your installation, email protect100for+huan@gmail.com. If possible, use the in-game data-request action before deleting the app so the message can include the reference needed to locate records. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify a request.

Depending on your location, applicable law may also give you rights to portability, restriction, objection, or a complaint to your local data-protection authority. Where consent is the basis for optional analytics, you can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. We use support information to respond to your request and protect the service, and may retain information where required by law.

7. Retention and security

Local data remains until it is reset or removed from your device. Analytics data is retained according to the configured Google Analytics retention settings and the need to evaluate gameplay and return visits. Retention can be affected by later activity; aggregate reports may be kept longer than individual event records. You can contact us for current retention details or request deletion of associated records.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests, follow up on issues, and meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by providers that support analytics, email, and website hosting, including outside your country. Their applicable service terms and safeguards govern that processing. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights or security. We do not use gameplay data for cross-company behavioral advertising.

9. This website

We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms, or cookies to this website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your own email application.

10. Children

Clay Unloop is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or where parental consent is required by local law, without that consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its information practices change. The latest version and effective date will appear here. For questions or requests, contact the Clay Unloop team at protect100for+huan@gmail.com.
